[Website](https://ysha.netlify.app/) · [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=Bg8TdxMAAAAJ) · [Email](mailto:yh2429@cornell.edu)

## Research interests

- **Simulation optimization:** Developing methods for optimizing complex systems using stochastic simulation.
- **Decision-focused learning:** Integrating learning and optimization, with an emphasis on the quality of downstream decisions.
- **Digital twins:** Connecting data, simulation, and optimization for real-time decision-making.
- **Quantum computing:** Optimization methods for quantum computing, including hybrid quantum–classical algorithms.
